How a line is validated

Every marketplace ships a vendor price. The engine computes our price from the item's MRP and the marketplace's margin, then compares. If they agree (within tolerance) the line is OK; if not, it's a MISMATCH for you to decide on.

Landing basis

Used by Blinkit, Flipkart, Myntra… The check is pre-GST.

Our Landing = MRP × margin%
Example — MRP ₹500, margin 70%
→ Our Landing = 500 × 0.70 = ₹350
Vendor landing ₹350 → OK · ₹380 → MISMATCH (diff ₹30)
Cost basis

Used by RK, Blink, Reliance… The check is post-GST (cost price).

Our CP = MRP × margin% ÷ (1 + GST)
Example — MRP ₹500, margin 70%, GST 18%
→ Our CP = 500 × 0.70 ÷ 1.18 = ₹296.61
Vendor CP ₹296.61 → OK · ₹236.69 → MISMATCH

Marketplace-specific exceptions — auto-detected; the engine tags exception_label

Not every line follows the flat marketplace margin. The engine recognises these named cases and prices / accepts them correctly — so a "deviation" here is expected, not an error. Don't assume the standard rate always applies.

{{ m.name }} · GST-dependent margin

{{ m.name }}'s keep% isn't flat — it changes with each item's GST rate: 1 − {{ m.gst_discount }} × (1 + GST). Lower GST → higher margin. The rules table shows the range {{ m.margin_label }} (marked by GST); {{ m.margin }}% is just the 18% case.

Blinkit · EPISENSE deal

Blinkit's EPISENSE promo prices a SKU off a deal MRP at a special landing%, not the master MRP at the flat 70%. Recorded as a price override so the line isn't flagged as a mismatch.

Deal MRP ₹899 @ 76% (a 24% discount) instead of the master's ₹1099 @ 70%. The engine expects the deal figure for that line.

Myntra · Goddess (Use Vendor CP)

For tagged items (e.g. RENEE Goddess perfume) the vendor's stated cost is authoritative: validation accepts it (no mismatch) and the D365 Unit Price is written from the vendor CP.

Myntra's Goddess line ships at the vendor's CP as-is — the engine doesn't force the standard margin.
EAN remap any marketplace

A punch's EAN is remapped to the correct master item (barcode changed / duplicate EAN) via the central Master Exceptions file.

Vendor EAN 8906121640885 → master 8906121640885_1 → resolves to the right item so the SO is correct.

These live in the engine config (Reliance) or the central Master Exceptions.xlsx / Swiggy Deal SKUs overlay — edit one place and every marketplace's output reflects it.

5

Your decisions on a mismatch — drive the D365 dump

When a line is MISMATCH, you pick one on the review screen. The full SO Workbook always keeps every line; only the D365 dump reflects your choice.

Include as-is

Ship it with our computed price; you accept the difference.

Our CP ₹147.71, theirs ₹236.69 → D365 line uses ₹147.71.
Override set CP

Include it, but with the CP you enter (the box pre-fills with vendor's Their CP, editable).

You set CP = ₹236.69 → D365 line Unit Price = ₹236.69.
Exclude drop

Leave the line out of the D365 dump entirely (still in the full workbook for audit).

Vendor price clearly wrong → excluded; ERP never sees it.

The DB keeps the original forever: status=MISMATCH, diff, plus your action / override_cp / remark / decided_at — so you always know it was problematic and what you did.
